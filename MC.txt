Question: Explain the architecture of 8051 Microcontroller.
    Reference: The 8051 microcontroller is an 8-bit architecture consisting of an 8-bit CPU, 4KB on-chip ROM, 128 bytes on-chip RAM, two 16-bit timers, four 8-bit I/O ports, and a serial communication interface.
    Keywords: 8-bit, CPU, ROM, RAM, timers, I/O ports, serial communication, architecture
